# bioreactor-kinetics-inversion

Fed-batch kinetic parameter estimation under a metabolic phase shift, followed by mass-balance-constrained dynamic feed optimization.

## Difficulty

All telemetry is synthetic but seeded from a stiff five-state fed-batch model with a discontinuous metabolic switch, sparse irregular sampling, and substantial sensor noise including imperfect feed measurements. In practice this class of inverse problem is handled by fermentation scientists and bioprocess engineers during strain/process characterization. Difficulty arises from jointly identifying non-smooth kinetics from limited noisy assays and then solving a constrained dynamic feed optimization whose feasible region is shaped by substrate inhibition and dissolved-oxygen floors.

## Reference solution

Fit the seven unknown kinetic parameters by simulating the Haldane–Andrews/product-inhibition ODEs against both historical runs (coarse grid over `t_switch` and `P_crit`, then multi-start least-squares, treating measured feed as a noisy zero-order-hold input and reading known `gamma` from reactor specs), then optimize a ten-interval piecewise-constant feed with SLSQP under volume, substrate, and DO penalties to maximize final product mass.

## Verification

Ground truth parameters and a reference feed were generated by independent Radau integration with a fixed seed in `authoring/provenance/`. The evaluation re-integrates proposed feeds under the true kinetics and enforces relative parameter tolerances (10% on `mu_max`/`P_crit`/`alpha`/`beta`, 20% on `K_S`/`K_I`, `t_switch` within 0.5 h), $ S \le 15 $ g/L (0.5 g/L numerical slack), $ C_L \ge 0.15\,C^{\star} $, $ V \le V_{\max} $, and minimum product mass 249.33 g. An independent reimplementation in `authoring/evidence/` recovered oracle score 1 and nop score 0 with matching constraint margins.
