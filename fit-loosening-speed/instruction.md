A short aluminum pulley is shrunk onto a hollow steel spindle and has to keep transmitting torque after the pulley warms up and the assembly spins. The pulley length is comparable to its wall thickness, so the contact pressure varies along the interface. A uniform long-cylinder pressure is the wrong description of this hub.

The numbers are in /app/data/problem.json. Radii and lengths are metres, temperatures are degrees Celsius, diametral_interference_m is the diametral overlap at the assembly temperature, and required_torque_nm is in newton-metres. The spindle fills r from shaft_inner_radius_m to shaft_outer_radius_m and z from 0 to shaft_length_m. The pulley fills r from shaft_outer_radius_m to sleeve_outer_radius_m and z from sleeve_z0_m over a length sleeve_length_m. Both parts are isotropic and linear elastic, with the constants under shaft and sleeve. Treat the bodies as axisymmetric in the r-z plane, and take the hoop strain as the outward radial displacement divided by the local radius.

At assembly_temperature_c both parts are stress-free before the interference is closed. The radial overlap that the stuck faces must accommodate is half of diametral_interference_m. After assembly there is no opening and no axial slip. Contact pressure is positive when it pushes the pulley bore outward. Rotation adds an outward body force equal to density times angular velocity squared times radius, using each part's own density. Thermal strain is isotropic, equal to alpha_per_c times the rise above assembly_temperature_c, using each part's own expansion coefficient and the temperature of the case being solved.

Solve two cases. The assembly case uses assembly_temperature_c in both parts and zero angular velocity. The operating case uses operating_shaft_temperature_c in the spindle, operating_sleeve_temperature_c in the pulley, and a common angular velocity. Torque capacity is friction_coefficient times the integral of contact pressure times shaft_outer_radius_m squared over the lateral area of the interface. At the operating temperatures the capacity is above required_torque_nm at 1000 rad/s and below it at 3000 rad/s, and the contact pressure stays compressive between those speeds. The limit speed is the angular velocity at which the operating torque capacity equals required_torque_nm.
