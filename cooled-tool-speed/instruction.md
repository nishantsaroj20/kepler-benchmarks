A carbide turning tool has a round coolant hole behind the cutting edge. At a stated cutting speed the rake face is hot, the edge grows toward the workpiece, and there is a higher speed at which the cutting corner reaches a temperature limit. The hole and the drop in conductivity as the carbide heats both matter. A solid tool, or one conductivity taken at room temperature, will not meet the stations.

The numbers are in /app/data/problem.json. Lengths are metres and temperatures are degrees Celsius. The cross-section is the rectangle 0 ≤ x ≤ width_m, 0 ≤ y ≤ height_m. The rake is the top edge y = height_m. Heat enters only on 0 ≤ x ≤ heat_patch_length_m. The coolant hole is a circle centered at (hole_center_x_m, hole_center_y_m) with radius hole_radius_m. The shank face x = width_m is held at shank_temperature_c.

Conductivity is conductivity_ref_w_mk divided by one plus conductivity_softening_per_k times the rise above conductivity_ref_temperature_c, and the rise is never taken as negative. The inward heat flux on the rake patch, in watts per square metre, is heat_flux_per_speed_w_m2_per_m_s times the cutting speed in metres per second. The hole wall loses heat by convection to coolant at coolant_temperature_c with coefficient coolant_h_w_m2k. Every other exposed face, including the flank x = 0 and the rake beyond the patch, loses heat by convection to air at air_temperature_c with coefficient air_h_w_m2k. Treat the section as plane strain and steady.

The same temperatures drive the elastic displacement. The carbide is isotropic, with young_modulus_pa, poisson_ratio, and alpha_per_c, and it is stress-free at stress_free_temperature_c. Use the plane-strain thermal strain alpha_per_c times the temperature rise times (1 + poisson_ratio). The shank face is clamped, so both displacement components are zero there. edge_growth_um is the displacement of the cutting corner (x = 0, y = height_m) in the negative-x direction, in micrometres, at reference_speed_m_s.

Report two speeds. At reference_speed_m_s, record the rake temperatures and the edge growth. The limit speed is the cutting speed at which the corner temperature equals edge_temperature_limit_c. That speed lies between speed_bracket_low_m_s and speed_bracket_high_m_s.

Write /app/output/tool_result.json. rake_temperature_c holds five temperatures in degrees Celsius on the heated rake, at 0.15, 0.35, 0.55, 0.75, and 0.95 of heat_patch_length_m. edge_growth_um is the corner growth defined above. limit_speed_m_s is the limit speed in metres per second. Every value must be finite. Each rake temperature must be within 3 percent of the converged value, the edge growth within 3 percent, and the limit speed within 2 percent.
