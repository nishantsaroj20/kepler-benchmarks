A short aluminum pulley is shrunk onto a hollow steel spindle. After the pulley warms and the assembly is spinning, the fit still has to carry a stated torque. The pulley is short relative to its wall, so the contact pressure changes along the length. One uniform thick-cylinder pressure will not do.

The numbers are in /app/data/problem.json. Lengths are metres, temperatures are degrees Celsius, the interference is diametral, and torque is in newton-metres. The spindle fills the radius from shaft_inner_radius_m to shaft_outer_radius_m and runs from z = 0 to shaft_length_m. The pulley bore is at shaft_outer_radius_m, the outside radius is sleeve_outer_radius_m, and the pulley starts at sleeve_z0_m with length sleeve_length_m. Modulus, Poisson's ratio, expansion coefficient, and density for each part are under shaft and sleeve.

Both parts are isotropic and linear elastic, in an axisymmetric r-z model. Hoop strain is the outward radial displacement divided by the local radius. Before the fit is closed, both parts are stress-free at assembly_temperature_c. The radial overlap is half of diametral_interference_m. After closure the faces stay shut and do not slip along z. Contact pressure is positive when it pushes the pulley bore outward. Spin adds an outward body force equal to that part's density times angular velocity squared times radius. Thermal strain is that part's alpha_per_c times its temperature rise above assembly_temperature_c.

There are two cases. For assembly, both parts are at assembly_temperature_c and the speed is zero. For operation, the spindle is at operating_shaft_temperature_c, the pulley is at operating_sleeve_temperature_c, and both spin at the same angular velocity. Torque capacity is friction_coefficient times the integral of contact pressure times shaft_outer_radius_m squared over the cylindrical interface. At the operating temperatures that capacity is above required_torque_nm at 1000 rad/s and below it at 3000 rad/s, and the whole interface stays in compression between those speeds. The limit speed is the angular velocity at which the operating capacity equals required_torque_nm.

Write /app/output/fit_result.json. assembly_pressure_pa holds five pressures in pascals, at 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, and 0.9 of the pulley length measured from the low-z end. assembly_torque_capacity_nm is the assembly torque in newton-metres. limit_speed_rad_s is the limit speed in radians per second. Every value must be finite. Each pressure must be within 3 percent of the converged axisymmetric pressure, the torque within 1.5 percent, and the limit speed within 2 percent.
